<h1 align="center">Hi 👋, I'm Sakar Shrestha</h1>
<h3 align="center">I'm a frontend developer who enjoys turning designs into responsive, working interfaces.

I mainly work with React and JavaScript, and I also have a background in WordPress, where I've worked on themes and Gutenberg plugins. I enjoy figuring out how things should behave beyond the design — responsive layouts, reusable components, interactions, and the little frontend problems that come up along the way.

Currently, I'm focusing on getting deeper into React and TypeScript and becoming a stronger frontend engineer. I also have some familiarity with backend technologies and enjoy understanding how the different pieces of an application fit together.

Lately, I've also been experimenting with AI agents and LLMs, mostly out of curiosity and to see where they can actually be useful in everyday development.</h3>
